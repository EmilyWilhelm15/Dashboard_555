# Exploring Variability of Personal Air Pollution Exposure Dashboard 


## Dashboard Overview
This dashboard explores personal exposure to fine particulate matter (PM₂.₅) among participants in the Atlanta, Georgia metropolitan area. It compares participant personal exposure data with ambient air quality measurements to better understand how real-world exposures differ from traditional ambient monitoring.

## Research Topic
This project focuses on environmental exposure science, specifically examining variability in personal PM₂.₅ exposure and how it aligns with EPA and WHO air quality guidelines. The goal is to identify patterns in exposure that may not be captured by ambient monitoring alone and to highlight public health implications of relying on ambient air monitoring data in research.

## Data Sources
- **Participant Data:** 48-hour continuous personal exposure monitoring using MicroPEM devices collected through Emory University’s Climate and Health Actionable Research and Translation Center (CHART) between May and August 2025. 
- **Ambient Data:** Georgia Department of Public Health (South DeKalb Ambient Air Monitor)
  https://airgeorgia.org/

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive boxplots comparing participant data categorized by EPA vs WHO PM₂.₅ guideline categories
- Histograms comparing personal PM₂.₅ with the corresponding ambient PM₂.₅ concentrations
- Interactive tooltips for detailed data exploration
